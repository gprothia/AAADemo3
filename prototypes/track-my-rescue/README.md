# Prototype: Track my rescue

**Built by:** Marketing, in Antigravity ("vibe-coded" in a few minutes).
**Status:** prototype only. Fake data, no backend, never deployed.

## The idea, in plain words
When members are stranded, the worst part is not knowing when help will arrive. Give them one
page that shows how far away the truck is, who is coming, and lets them reassure family.

## What the page shows
- A big ETA ("18 min") that counts down and refreshes every few seconds
- Progress: Received → Truck assigned → On the way → Arrived
- A small map with the truck moving towards the member
- The driver: name, truck, rating, number of rescues and phone number
- Buttons: **Call driver**, **Share with family** (a link anyone can open), **Cancel request**
